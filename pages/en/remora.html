The idea

A remora is a fish that attaches itself to a larger animal and travels wherever it goes. Remora the drone does the same with a person: it follows on its own, without anyone steering it.

It is designed in modules, so that the frame, the sensors and the payload can be changed without redesigning the rest. The project will be documented here as it happens, decisions and failures included.

The core problem

Following someone is a control problem. Above, the drone is pulled toward a point just behind the person by a spring and slowed by a damper, the simplest controller there is. Too little damping and it overshoots and wobbles; too much and it lags behind. Real hardware adds sensor delay, wind and limited thrust, so finding the right trade-off, and measuring it, is a large part of this project.
